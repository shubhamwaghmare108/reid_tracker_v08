============================================================
2. SEPARATE TRACKING FROM IDENTITY RECOGNITION
============================================================

Maintain the existing use of body Re-ID for TRACK ASSOCIATION.

Body Re-ID is useful for:

    detection
       ↓
    same physical track?

That does NOT mean body Re-ID should automatically answer:

    who is this person?

Separate:

TRACK MATCHING
IDENTITY RECOGNITION
IDENTITY RECOVERY

These are different decisions.

Do not weaken the existing tracker association just to fix
identity recognition.

============================================================
3. FACE SHOULD BE THE PRIMARY KNOWN-IDENTITY EVIDENCE
============================================================

For a known identity such as "shubham":

If a reliable face embedding is available:

    face recognition
          ↓
    strong face match
          ↓
    known identity

Face evidence should have substantially greater authority than
body-only evidence for declaring a known identity.

Body Re-ID should support the decision.

Do not allow a weak body similarity to override a strong conflicting
face result.

============================================================
4. BODY-ONLY MATCH MUST BE CONSERVATIVE
============================================================

This is the most important requirement.

When:

    face = unavailable

and:

    body similarity to "shubham" = moderately/high

DO NOT immediately assign:

    identity = shubham

Instead use:

    identity_candidate = shubham

and require additional evidence.

Possible evidence:

- repeated consistent body matches
- same canonical track history
- stable trajectory
- previous confirmed identity
- high-quality body embedding
- multiple gallery samples/prototypes
- temporal consistency
- no competing known identity
- no simultaneous canonical owner for that identity

Use configurable thresholds.

Do not simply lower or raise one global threshold.

============================================================
5. INTRODUCE IDENTITY STATES
============================================================

If the architecture does not already have an equivalent mechanism,
introduce identity state separately from TrackState.

For example:

    UNKNOWN
    CANDIDATE
    CONFIRMED
    RETAINED

Meaning:

UNKNOWN:
    No reliable known identity.

CANDIDATE:
    Body/face evidence suggests a possible known identity but
    evidence is not yet sufficient.

CONFIRMED:
    Identity has sufficient reliable evidence.

RETAINED:
    Identity was previously confirmed and is temporarily being
    retained through occlusion/face disappearance.

Do NOT confuse this with:

TENTATIVE
CONFIRMED
OCCLUDED
RECOVERED
LOST

Track state and identity state are separate concepts.

============================================================
6. IDENTITY HYSTERESIS
============================================================

Do not change identity from:

    UNKNOWN → shubham

because of one good body embedding.

Require temporal consistency.

Example:

Frame 1:
    body candidate = shubham

Frame 2:
    body candidate = shubham

Frame 3:
    body candidate = shubham

Frame 4:
    strong evidence

Then:

    identity = shubham

Similarly, once a person is confirmed as shubham:

    shubham
       ↓
    temporary weak body evidence
       ↓
    still shubham

Do not immediately change identity because one frame is ambiguous.

Use configurable parameters such as:

- identity_candidate_min_frames
- identity_confirm_threshold
- identity_confirm_margin
- identity_switch_threshold
- identity_switch_min_frames
- identity_retention_frames

Use names consistent with the existing Settings architecture.

============================================================
7. KNOWN IDENTITY EXCLUSIVITY
============================================================

Enforce:

    ONE KNOWN IDENTITY
           ↓
    ONE CANONICAL OWNER

Example:

Track 6:
    strong face match
    identity = shubham

Track 9:
    body similarity = shubham candidate

Expected:

Track 6 → shubham
Track 9 → UNKNOWN/CANDIDATE

NOT:

Track 6 → shubham
Track 9 → shubham

unless Track 9 has overwhelmingly stronger independent evidence.

If two tracks claim the same known identity:

1. Compare face evidence.
2. Compare identity confidence.
3. Compare historical consistency.
4. Compare number of stable observations.
5. Compare body/face quality.
6. Prefer the existing canonical owner when evidence is ambiguous.
7. Keep the weaker track anonymous rather than forcing identity.

Do not merge physical tracks solely because they claim the same name.

============================================================
8. CANONICAL IDENTITY OWNER
============================================================

Maintain a canonical mapping conceptually like:

    shubham → canonical_track_id

If the implementation already has an equivalent mechanism,
reuse it rather than creating unnecessary duplication.

The important invariant is:

    one physical known identity
        ↓
    one canonical identity owner

A track ID may change during recovery, but the canonical person
identity must remain stable.

============================================================
9. RECOVERY MUST PRESERVE CONFIRMED IDENTITY
============================================================

Example:

Frame 100:

    Track 6
    face match
    shubham CONFIRMED

Frames 101–130:

    face unavailable
    person becomes OCCLUDED

Frame 131:

    Track 6 or Track 9 recovered
    body similarity supports previous track

Expected:

    shubham
    same canonical identity

NOT:

    OUT
    IN
    unknown
    new shubham

Do not require a new face recognition event if a previously
confirmed identity has strong temporal/body continuity.

However, do not let a completely unrelated track inherit the
identity merely because its body embedding is moderately similar.

============================================================
10. IDENTITY RECOVERY SHOULD USE TRACK HISTORY
============================================================

For a previously confirmed identity, compare the current embedding
against:

- known identity gallery
- historical track body gallery
- historical track face gallery when available
- recent stable embeddings
- trajectory/temporal context

Prefer consistency with the person's own recent track history.

Conceptually:

current embedding
      ↓
historical track gallery
      +
known identity gallery
      +
motion/temporal consistency
      ↓
identity recovery decision

Do not rely exclusively on one current-frame embedding.

============================================================
11. DO NOT LET LOW-CONFIDENCE MATCHES CONTAMINATE GALLERIES
============================================================

Inspect every gallery update.

Separate:

TRACKING CONFIDENCE

from:

IDENTITY CONFIDENCE

A track can have:

    excellent tracking confidence
    but incorrect identity

Therefore:

    tracking_score >= threshold

must NOT automatically mean:

    safe identity-gallery update

Only update a known identity's gallery using sufficiently reliable
identity evidence.

Do NOT update a known identity gallery from:

- UNKNOWN tracks
- ambiguous candidates
- tentative tracks
- weak body-only identity matches
- OCCLUDED predictions
- LOST tracks
- identity-conflict tracks
- newly recovered tracks with weak evidence
- low-quality face detections
- low-quality body crops

Use strict identity-quality gates.

============================================================
14. FACE QUALITY GATING
============================================================

Do not treat every detected face equally.

Before using face evidence, consider:

- face confidence
- face bounding-box size
- blur/quality if already available
- frontal/profile quality if available
- embedding validity
- similarity margin

A tiny/blurred/partial face should not override reliable historical
identity evidence.

Likewise, a strong face match should dominate a weak body conflict.

============================================================
16. IDENTITY DECISION LOGIC
============================================================

Implement a decision system conceptually like:

                    CURRENT TRACK
                         │
                         ▼
                  identity manager
                         │
              ┌──────────┴──────────┐
              │                     │
        face available         no face
              │                     │
              ▼                     ▼
       face recognition        body candidate
              │                     │
       ┌──────┴──────┐        temporal evidence
       │             │              │
     strong        weak             ▼
       │             │        candidate/unknown
       ▼             ▼
   confirm       use history
       │
       └──────────────┬─────────────┘
                      ▼
             canonical identity
```

Never make:

    body_similarity >= 0.55

(or any single arbitrary threshold)

equivalent to:

    known identity confirmed

without considering modality, history, margin, and identity
ownership.

============================================================
17. IDENTITY MARGIN
============================================================

Do not accept the best gallery identity solely because it is the
highest score.

Require:

    best_score >= minimum_threshold

AND, where applicable:

    best_score - second_best_score >= margin

This prevents ambiguous body embeddings from being assigned to a
known identity.

If the identity is ambiguous:

    UNKNOWN

is preferable to:

    WRONG KNOWN IDENTITY

============================================================
18. OPEN-SET BEHAVIOR
============================================================

The system must support:

    "I don't know this person."

This is essential.

Unknown people should remain:

    UNKNOWN

when evidence is insufficient.

Do NOT force every detection into one of the enrolled identities.

The desired behavior is:

    known → known
    unknown → unknown
    ambiguous → unknown/candidate

not:

    ambiguous → nearest known person

============================================================
19. IDENTITY SWITCH PROTECTION
============================================================

Once:

    Track 6 = shubham

has been strongly confirmed, do not switch:

    Track 6 = another identity

because of one ambiguous frame.

Require strong contradictory evidence over multiple observations.

Likewise, don't allow:

    Track 9 = shubham

to steal the identity from Track 6 because of one body match.

Identity changes should be rare and evidence-driven.
